A slot machine where skill matters

Every pull is in your hands. Time the lever, steer the reels and build a machine the house never saw coming.

  1. Pull with skill

    Hold the lever and let go in the green for a Perfect Pull. Hit the gold Bullseye dead center for even more. String them together to catch fire: ON FIRE, then BLUE FIRE, then WHITE HOT.

  2. Nudge the reels

    A Perfect Pull earns a nudge and a Bullseye earns three. After the reels stop, slide one a step to line up the win.

  3. Customize your machine

    Rebuild your reels in the Workshop by adding, removing and gilding symbols. Pick a machine that suits your style.

  4. Buy upgrades

    Raise your bet, light up more paylines, add a Magnet for luck or an Auto-Spinner to work the lever for you.

  5. Overcome the challenges

    Every casino sets a target to beat. Random events, challenges and jinxes come up every few spins. Run out of chips and it's over.

  6. Eight casinos

    Start at the Rusty Nickel and work your way down the Strip. Each casino has its own twist and a High Roller Table bonus round.
