Privacy Policy

Tall Order, by Unicorn Talent LLC. Effective 15 October 2026.

We built Tall Order to collect nothing about you. We don't run ads, analytics or tracking, and the game has no accounts of its own.

What stays on your iPhone

Your progress (levels, unlocks, streaks, missions, settings and best scores) is saved only on your device. We never receive it.

Game Center

Leaderboards, achievements and Battles use Apple's Game Center. When you sign in to Game Center, your Game Center name, scores and achievements are shared through Apple's service with other players, as Game Center does in every game. During a Battle, the other players' devices receive your Game Center name, what you're wearing in the game and your moves, so they can see your tower. This goes through Apple's servers and is not stored by us. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game which foods you own, so it can unlock them.

Children

Tall Order is rated 4+ and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
