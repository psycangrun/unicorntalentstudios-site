A layer for every player

Tap to drop and stack it sky high! 16 cute foods, a new Daily every day, and live Battles for up to 16 players. Miss once and you're out: last tower standing wins.

Features

One tap, one layer, one very tall order. Whatever hangs over the edge falls off, so every layer is a little narrower. Land it dead center for a PERFECT and watch your tower climb into the clouds.

  1. Timed challenges

    Classic gives you 60 seconds to stack as high as you can, and a new Daily Challenge lands every day.

  2. Marathon play

    No clock. Stack until you miss.

  3. 1v1 Battles

    Best of three rounds against a friend or a random opponent.

  4. 16-player rumbles

    Up to 16 players at once. Last tower standing wins.

  5. Choose your food

    Every food has its own cute layers, its own base and its own mastery tiers, from Silver to Rainbow.

  6. Daily Missions

    Three new missions every day, plus a monthly exclusive.
