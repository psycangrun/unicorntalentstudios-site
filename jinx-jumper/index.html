Slightly spooky, mostly fun

A one-thumb runner down a night street full of superstitions. Every jinx you hit costs luck, and when your luck runs out, so does your run.

  1. Tap to jump

    Tap to jump and tap again in the air to double jump. Hop onto awnings, fire escapes and rooftops.

  2. Swipe to slide

    Swipe down to go low under crows, umbrellas and bats. Hold it to keep sliding; swipe in the air to slam back down.

  3. Luck, not lives

    Stay clean and your luck comes back. Drop below 30 and you're Unlucky: the street darkens and the jinxes come thicker.

  4. Seven years bad luck

    Break a mirror and you're cursed for seven seconds ("years"), with your luck draining away.

  5. Two ways to run

    Classic gives you 60 seconds for a high score. Marathon keeps going, and speeding up, until your luck runs out.

  6. A street that never repeats

    The run is built a stretch at a time: open sidewalk, ledges, penny-lined bonus runs and rooftop gaps.

Jinxes

Nine superstitions stand between you and a new best run. Each one has its own way out.

  • Jump

    Black cat

    Walks straight toward you.

  • Jump

    Ladder

    Running under it is the jinx. Clear it with a well-timed full jump.

  • Jump

    Broken mirror

    Falls from a ledge as you come close and leaves glass behind. And a curse.

  • Hop

    Sidewalk cracks

    One to three in a row, and they only count if you step on them.

  • Slide

    Open umbrella

    Hangs from a clothesline. Too wide to jump, so get low.

  • Duck

    Crow

    Swoops in fast at head height.

  • Jump

    Spilled salt

    A slippery patch that makes you skid faster.

  • Slide

    Bats

    A swarm too tall to jump and too low to duck.

  • Double jump

    Falling piano

    Watch its shadow grow. The wreck is too tall for one jump.

Lucky charms

Grab them along the way to turn your luck around.

  • 🍀

    Four-leaf clover

    A shield that blocks your next jinx.

  • 🧲

    Horseshoe

    Pulls in pennies and charms for a few seconds.

  • 🐇

    Rabbit's foot

    A triple jump: one extra jump in the air.

  • 🪵

    Knock on wood

    Save it, then KNOCK to clear every jinx on screen.

  • 🧂

    Salt over the shoulder

    Lifts the curse and gives you back some luck.

  • 🤞

    Crossed fingers

    Slows the whole street down for a moment.

Neighborhoods

Every half a minute the street turns into somewhere new, each with its own jinxes and its own signature trick to master.

  • Main Street

    The night city, and a balanced warm-up.

  • The Skyline

    Rooftops and climbs. Ride the window-washer lift across the gap.

  • The Graveyard

    Misty tombstones and crypts. Time your way past the bobbing ghost.

  • The Carnival

    Bonus pennies galore. A bounce pad throws you up onto the big top.

  • The Haunted Mansion

    Mirrors, pianos and a swinging chandelier that skims the floor.

  • The Rail Yard

    When the signal flashes, get up on the Platform 13 footbridge before the train.
