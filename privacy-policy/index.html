Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: our games and this website don't collect personal information about you. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no accounts of our own.

This policy covers the games made by Unicorn Talent LLC ("Unicorn Talent Studios", "we", "us") and the website at unicorntalentstudios.com. Some games also have their own privacy policy, linked below, which goes into more detail about that game.

Our games

  • Your progress stays on your device. Scores, unlocks, settings and other progress are saved only on your device. We never receive it, and it's removed when you delete the game.
  • No personal details. We don't ask for your name, email address or any other personal information, and we don't collect usage data, device identifiers or location.
  • No ads or tracking. Our games don't show ads, use advertising or analytics services, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • Game Center. Some of our games use Apple's Game Center for leaderboards, achievements and multiplayer. If you sign in to Game Center, your Game Center name, scores and achievements are shared through Apple's service with other players, and in multiplayer games the other players' devices receive what they need to show your moves. This goes through Apple and is not stored by us. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are made through Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game what you've bought so it can unlock it.

This website

This website has no cookies, no analytics, no sign-ups and no forms. Two outside services help deliver it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to run and protect its service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the site's fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the fonts from Google, which receives your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Gameplay videos and images on this site are served from the site itself.

If you email us

If you write to support@unicorntalentstudios.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you. We don't add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone. Ask us and we'll delete your messages.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Tall Order is rated 4+. Clinkerton contains simulated gambling and is meant for adults.

Your choices

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete beyond any emails you've sent us. You can remove a game's saved progress at any time by deleting the game from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future game adds a feature that handles personal information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@unicorntalentstudios.com.
