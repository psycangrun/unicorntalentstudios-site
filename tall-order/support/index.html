Tall Order Support

Something not working, or an idea for the game? Email us at support@unicorntalentstudio.com and we'll get back to you. It helps to include your iPhone model and what you were doing when it happened.

I bought a food but it isn't unlocked

Open Style, go to the Food tab and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they come back on a new phone or after reinstalling the game.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can ask for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Battles won't connect

Battles, leaderboards and achievements use Game Center. Make sure you're signed in under Settings → Game Center and have a connection. Invites only work between Game Center friends; to play anyone, use Find a match. You can always practise against Chef Bot without signing in.

My progress

Your levels, unlocks and streaks are saved on your iPhone. Deleting the game deletes them, except purchases, which you can restore.
